2. Our artisanal promise

Every confection, candle, and botanical stem comes from verified Indian independent makers. Fragile glass items receive a 1.25× honeycomb buffer during 3D packing validation, and zero orders leave our desk without passing our photographic QC gate.

3. Delivery & collection

Orders placed before 14:00 IST dispatch the same studio cycle. Express Air reaches Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune in 1–2 business days, and Tier-1/2 pin codes across India in 3–4 days. Shipping is complimentary on orders ₹2,999+.
