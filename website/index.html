How quickly do hampers dispatch across India?

Ready studio edits dispatch within 24 hours (Mon–Sat), reaching Delhi-NCR/Mumbai/Bengaluru in 1–2 days and rest of India in 3–4 days via BlueDart & Delhivery Express.

✦

Can I pick my ribbon, wax seal, and write a handwritten note?

Yes — every ready box lets you select raw silk ribbon & wax seal crest, and our 11-step Bespoke Atelier adds custom monograms, Polaroid prints, and live SVG previews.

✦

Do you handle wedding favors and corporate bulk orders?

Yes! From 10 executive kits to 500 wedding trousseau hampers, we offer quiet monogramming, GST invoicing, and multi-address dispatch.
